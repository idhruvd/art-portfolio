About Ishita

I grew up in Mumbai and now live and paint in Grand Rapids, Michigan. I'm a chartered accountant by profession, but painting has always been my calling, the way I express what I feel.

What moves me to paint is emotion, and the beauty that's easy to miss these days. Some pieces are an explosion of colour; others hold to a limited palette, which I find just as thrilling. I work in mixed media and keep experimenting with technique, brushstroke and texture.

I don't tie myself to one genre, which is why Warli folk scenes hang beside autumn streets and a pour of red wine. I'm still learning with every canvas, and I enjoy every moment of it.
